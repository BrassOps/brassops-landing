Privacy Policy

How BrassOps collects, uses, and protects your information.

Effective Date: April 1, 2026

1. Introduction

BrassOps ("we," "our," or "us") operates the brassops.com website and the BrassOps range intelligence platform (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service.

By accessing or using the Service, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, department/agency name, role, and contact information when you create an account or submit a contact form.
  • Training Data: Officer qualification scores, remedial training records, coaching notes, dry-fire session logs, and other training-related data entered into the platform by authorized users.
  • Armory Data: Weapon inventory records including serial numbers, inspection logs, repair history, and officer assignments.
  • Media Uploads: Target photographs uploaded for AI shot analysis.
  • Communications: Messages, feedback, and correspondence you send to us.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, time spent on the platform, and interaction patterns.
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device type, and screen resolution.
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, and referring URLs.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service
  • Process and track firearms qualifications, remedial training, and officer readiness
  • Deliver AI-powered shot analysis and performance analytics
  • Send automated alerts and notifications related to qualification expirations, remedial milestones, and officer status changes
  • Generate anonymized, aggregate data for the N.A.R.M.S. benchmarking system (no individually identifiable data is shared)
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Improve and develop new features for the Service
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or training data to third parties. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Within Your Agency: Training data is accessible to authorized users within your department based on their assigned role and permissions.
  • Service Providers: We use third-party service providers for hosting, email delivery, and analytics. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only to perform services on our behalf.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, court order, or government request.
  • Aggregated Data: We may share anonymized, aggregated data that cannot be used to identify any individual officer or agency (e.g., national benchmarking statistics through N.A.R.M.S.).

Your agency's data is fully isolated. No other agency or department can access your records. Period.

5. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously and implement the following measures:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit using TLS and at rest using AES-256 encryption.
  • Access Controls: Role-based access controls ensure users only see information appropriate to their role (officer, instructor, range master, command staff, admin).
  • Audit Logging: Every action within the platform is logged with timestamps for accountability and compliance.
  • Redundancy: Data is stored across multiple servers with automated backups to prevent loss.
  • U.S.-Based Hosting: All data is stored on secure, U.S.-based cloud infrastructure. Your data never leaves the country.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your agency maintains an active subscription. Training records and qualification history are preserved for the duration of your account to maintain audit-ready compliance records.

Upon cancellation, we provide a full data export so you retain all your records. After a reasonable transition period (90 days), your data is permanently deleted from our systems unless retention is required by law.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you or your agency.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Export: Request your data in a portable, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to certain processing activities.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below.

8. Cookies and Tracking

The BrassOps website uses minimal cookies necessary for the Service to function. We use Vercel Analytics for anonymous, aggregated website traffic analysis. We do not use third-party advertising trackers or sell browsing data.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is designed for use by law enforcement professionals and is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify active users of material changes via email or in-app notification. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
