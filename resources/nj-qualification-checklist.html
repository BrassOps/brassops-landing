New Jersey Firearms Qualification Checklist

The New Jersey Attorney General semi-annual qualification requirements as a one-page checklist: courses, standards, documentation fields, remedial steps, retention.

PDF, One page
From BrassOps
Updated October 2026

What is inside

  • The courses of fire the Attorney General requires, day and night, and how often each one has to run.
  • The passing standards for each course, so nobody has to look them up on the firing line.
  • Every documentation field a qualification record needs before it is complete: officer, weapon and serial, course, score, instructor, date.
  • The remedial steps that follow a failed attempt, and the deadline that applies.
  • The retention period, and who signs off on the record.
