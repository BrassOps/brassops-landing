The Platform

What Is BrassOps?

Most departments still manage firearms qualifications with binders, spreadsheets, and institutional memory. When an officer's certification lapses or a training deficiency goes unaddressed, the consequences can be serious — legally, operationally, and personally.

BrassOps changes that. We are a modern, web-based platform that gives range masters, training coordinators, and department leadership a real-time picture of officer readiness. Extensive automations eliminate manual busywork — from qualification tracking and remedial enrollment to alert notifications — while comprehensive training documentation ensures every session, score, and coaching note is captured and audit-ready. From logging qualification scores to managing remedial training workflows to analyzing shot patterns with AI — everything lives in one place, accessible from any device.

Whether you run a 10-officer department or a 100,000-officer agency, BrassOps scales to fit your program and keeps your team accountable, compliant, and mission-ready.

Capabilities

Everything Your Range Program Needs

Built by people who understand the range. Designed for the teams who run it.

Qualification Tracking

Log sessions, record scores, and automatically track compliance against your agency's qualification requirements. Full audit trail, zero guesswork.

Officer Readiness Monitoring

Know at a glance which officers are mission-ready, developing, or at risk — before it becomes a problem. Color-coded dashboards make status unmistakable.

Remedial Training Management

Structured remedial workflows with issue tracking, coaching notes, and escalation paths. Document every step for complete defensibility.

Auto Remedial Enrollment

Officers who fall below qualification thresholds are automatically enrolled in remedial training. No manual intervention, no missed cases — the system handles it the moment a score is logged.

Performance Analytics

Trend data, department-wide statistics, and per-officer history that tells the full story. Identify patterns, measure progress, and make decisions backed by data. Department drill leaderboards and achievement badges add a competitive edge — motivating officers to stay sharp and giving top performers the recognition they've earned.

AI-Powered Shot Analysis

Upload a target photo and receive an instant diagnostic on shot grouping, probable cause, and recommended corrections. Like having an expert coach on every lane.

Dry-Fire Training

Log and track dry-fire sessions to maintain readiness between range days. Build consistent habits with structured practice that gets recorded and measured. Unlock achievement badges as you hit milestones — keeping training engaging and giving officers something to earn along the way.

Instructor Tools

Assign instructors, track coaching history, and measure officer progress over time. Give your training cadre the visibility they need to be effective.

Automated Alerts

Get notified when officers are flagged, qualifications are lapsing, or remedial cases need attention. The system watches so you don't have to.

Officer Behavioral Scoring

Track individual officer and agency-wide strengths and weaknesses over time with behavioral scoring. Pinpoint skill gaps, surface patterns, and use the data to design targeted trainings that address real deficiencies — not just check a box.

Range Ops

Manage range scheduling, range automations, inventory, and day-of logistics from one place. Streamline the operational side of your range so your team can focus on training.

Armory

Full weapon inventorying with serial number tracking, assigned officer records, inspection scheduling, and repair history. Know the status of every firearm in your fleet — from issue to retirement.

Community

BrassLink

BrassLink is the community backbone of BrassOps — built by instructors, for instructors. It's where firearms training professionals from agencies across the country come together to share knowledge, challenge assumptions, and push each other to be better. Every resource, review, and discussion is community-driven, because the best way to raise the standard of instruction nationwide is to give the people doing the work a platform to learn from one another.

Data

The National Anonymous Range Metrics System. Community-powered benchmarking and trend analysis aggregated across participating agencies — giving instructors real data to measure against and raising the bar for training programs everywhere.
