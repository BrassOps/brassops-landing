How Officers Get Better

Most Systems Record a Score. BrassOps Improves It.

A pass/fail column tells you an officer qualified. It does not tell you what went wrong, why, or what to do about it. BrassOps closes that gap with a loop that runs on every session, for every officer.

1

Measure

Every session captures accuracy, time, and hit-factor scoring, plus nine range behaviors: muzzle discipline, reload habits, response to coaching, and more. Not just whether they passed.

2

Diagnose

Photograph the target and AI finds every shot, then the app diagnoses the group pattern against national base rates. Two readiness engines watch practice accuracy and qualification performance, flagging score drops, repeat failures, and safety concerns.

3

Prescribe

The app recommends the drill that fixes the pattern it found. Instructors assign dry-fire work officers complete between range days, and a failed qualification opens a coaching plan with milestones automatically.

4

Verify

The next session shows whether it worked. Per-officer trends and department-wide weakness rollups prove the training changed something, instead of proving it merely happened.

Every trip through the loop makes the next one sharper. Officers see their own progress in My Performance, instructors walk onto the range already knowing each officer's gaps, and command staff can see which weaknesses are department-wide rather than individual. The documentation builds itself along the way.

The Platform

What Is BrassOps?

Most departments still manage firearms qualifications with binders, spreadsheets, and institutional memory. When an officer's certification lapses or a training deficiency goes unaddressed, the consequences can be serious, legally, operationally, and personally.

BrassOps changes that. We are a modern, web-based platform that gives rangemasters, training coordinators, and department leadership a real-time picture of officer readiness. Extensive automations eliminate manual busywork, from qualification tracking and remedial enrollment to alert notifications, while comprehensive training documentation ensures every session, score, and coaching note is captured and audit-ready. From logging qualification scores to managing remedial training workflows to AI-assisted target analysis, everything lives in one place, accessible from any device.

Whether you run a 10-officer department or a 100,000-officer agency, BrassOps scales to fit your program and keeps your team accountable, compliant, and mission-ready.

Capabilities

Everything Your Range Program Needs

Built by people who understand the range. Designed for the teams who run it.

Build Better Shooters

The capabilities that turn range time into measurable improvement, not just a completed record.

Officer Readiness

Every officer gets a readiness score and a clear grade from Elite Ready to Critical. One engine watches practice accuracy and another watches qualification performance, automatically flagging score drops, repeat failures, and safety concerns.

Remedial Training

The moment an officer fails a qualification, BrassOps opens a remedial case, builds a step-by-step coaching plan with milestones, and escalates to a supervisor and then command on a timer. The paper trail builds itself.

Analytics

Department accuracy, trends, rankings, cohorts, readiness funnels, instructor effectiveness, equipment reliability, and cost per qualified officer. See the whole program at a glance. Department drill leaderboards and achievement badges add a competitive edge that keeps officers sharp.

Target Analysis

Point your camera at the target. Photograph it and BrassOps uses AI to find every shot. The app then diagnoses the group pattern and recommends the drill to fix it, measured against national base rates. Camera based, no scoring hardware required.

Dry-Fire Training

Log and track dry-fire sessions to maintain readiness between range days. Build consistent habits with structured practice that gets recorded and measured. Unlock achievement badges as you hit milestones, keeping training engaging and giving officers something to earn along the way.

Behavioral Scoring

Score more than the target. Rate nine range behaviors every session, from muzzle discipline to reload habits to response to coaching. Roll them up into department weaknesses and per-officer trends.

Run the Program

Everything else it takes to operate a firearms program end to end, from the armory to the after-action report.

Qualification Tracking

Set your own standards per platform, cadence, attempts, and minimum score, and BrassOps tracks every officer against them. The Qualification Lapse board shows exactly who is current, who is due, and who has fallen out.

Session Logging

Log the whole relay, not one officer at a time. Log a single officer through a full-screen wizard, or score an entire relay in one batch screen. Time, accuracy, USPSA hit-factor and pass-fail scoring, multi-stage drills, and per-session behavior ratings. Built for a tablet on the firing line, gloves on.

Instructor Tools

Assign instructors, track coaching history, and measure officer progress over time. Give your training cadre the visibility they need to be effective.

Automated Alerts

Get notified when officers are flagged, qualifications are lapsing, or remedial cases need attention. The system watches so you don't have to.

Range Operations

Run a range day end to end. Schedule the day, take sign-ups, build firing relays, check people in, reconcile ammunition drawn against fired and returned, and generate the after-action report automatically.

Armory

Every weapon, every round, accounted for. Track weapons through their entire life: assignments, attachments and build sheets, holsters, inspections, repairs and work orders, parts, schematics, and disposition. Round-life alerts tell you when a weapon is due for service.

Ammunition and Lot Tracking

Track ammunition by lot with full purchase, storage, and usage history. When a lot is recalled, one click quarantines it and shows every officer, session, and weapon that touched it. Scan an ammo box with your camera and the label fills itself in.

Reports and Records

Court-ready and audit-ready, on demand. Eighteen report types, from a court-ready officer training record to an audit-ready qualification status report, plus annual firearms reporting and full data export. Officers acknowledge policies with real, timestamped e-signatures.

Shot Timer

A shot timer in your pocket. Microphone-based shot detection that rejects its own start beep. No extra hardware to buy.

Specialist Training

Beyond the qualification line. Dedicated modules for active-shooter response scenarios with scored team runs, Taser certification tracking, and a precision-rifle log with a full ballistics solver.

Automation & AI

Automation That Gives the Time Back to Training

BrassOps puts AI to work on the busywork, not the judgment calls. Photograph an ammo box and the label fills itself in. Finish a range day and generate a full narrative after-action report and the next lesson plan from the day's data. Photograph a target and the app finds every shot automatically. An AI help assistant answers questions inside the app, and automatic alerts flag lapses, service-due weapons, and flagged officers before they become problems.

Privacy by design. Officer identities are stripped and replaced before anything is sent to the AI, then restored afterward. Every agency sets a monthly AI spending cap and can switch AI off entirely.

Security & Trust

Built for Agencies That Answer to Oversight

Your data is sensitive. We treat it that way. BrassOps is engineered to meet the security demands of public safety agencies.

Mandatory Two-Factor

Two-factor authentication is required for every user: passkey, authenticator app, or email, with recovery codes. Repeated failed sign-ins trigger automatic lockout.

Full Agency Data Isolation

Each agency's data is fully isolated by database-level row security. One agency can never see another's records.

Single Sign-On

Google Workspace single sign-on for command staff, with role-based permissions so each user sees only what they are authorized to access.

Privacy by Design

Officer identities are stripped and replaced before anything is sent to AI, then restored afterward. Every agency sets a monthly AI spending cap and can switch AI off entirely.
