Platform Capabilities
Everything Your Range Program Needs
Every feature is designed to save time, reduce risk, or create defensible documentation. Most do all three.
Qualification Tracking
Log every session with scores, weapon, course of fire, and instructor. Pass/fail auto-calculated against your thresholds with automatic expiration tracking.
Officer Readiness Dashboard
Live color-coded view of every officer: green (qualified), yellow (expiring), red (non-compliant). Filter by unit, shift, or rank.
Auto Remedial Enrollment
The moment a score fails, a remedial case is created with a structured workflow — coaching plan, milestones, and escalation paths. Zero missed cases.
AI-Powered Shot Analysis
Snap a target photo from your phone. AI identifies grouping patterns, probable cause, and recommends corrective drills. Results saved to the officer's profile.
Performance Analytics
Per-officer score trends and department-wide statistics. Behavioral scoring pinpoints skill gaps. Drill leaderboards and badges drive engagement.
Automated Alerts
Configurable warnings at 90, 60, and 30 days before qualifications expire. Remedial milestone alerts and escalation notifications sent automatically.
Armory Inventory
Every firearm tracked by serial number with make, model, acquisition date, and officer assignment. Full lifecycle management from issue to retirement.
Weapon Inspections & Repairs
Schedule inspections with due-date alerts. Log completions and maintain a full repair history tied to each firearm.
Weapon Performance Monitor
Link qualification data to specific weapons by serial. Track per-weapon accuracy trends, round counts, and reliability. Flags firearms with declining performance.
Officer / Instructor Pairing
Match officers with instructors based on skill gaps and specialties. Same instructor follows through remedial to re-qualification for coaching continuity.
Instructor Tools
Assign instructors to sessions and remedial cases. Log coaching notes tied to officers. Full roster and workload visibility for training coordinators.
Range Ops
Range day scheduling, ammo and supply inventory, configurable automations for recurring workflows, and day-of logistics — all in one place.
Dry-Fire Training
Officers log structured dry-fire practice between range days. Achievement badges unlock at milestones to keep training consistent and engaging.
BrassLink Community
Built-in instructor network with anonymous cross-agency benchmarking (N.A.R.M.S.), shared training resources, peer reviews, and equipment assessments.

Legal Liability
The 5 Documentation Failures That Lose Lawsuits
These are the gaps that consistently expose agencies in court. Every one is preventable.
Failure
1. Missing Qualification Records
Agency can't prove the officer was current at the time of an incident. Attorneys exploit the gap — juries notice.
How BrassOps Solves It
Every qual auto-logged
Date, score, weapon, course of fire, and instructor — time-stamped and tamper-proof. Proves exactly when each officer qualified.
Failure
2. No Remedial Training Logs
Officer failed or struggled — but no follow-up documented. The agency knew about the deficiency and did nothing. That's negligence.
How BrassOps Solves It
Auto remedial with full trail
Remedial case created instantly on failure. Every coaching session, milestone, and escalation logged with timestamps.
Failure
3. Incomplete Lesson Plans
Department can't show what was actually taught. Without documented curriculum, there's no way to prove training was appropriate.
How BrassOps Solves It
Sessions tied to training plans
Every session linked to specific courses of fire. Instructors log what was covered, drills conducted, and coaching notes.
Failure
4. No Attendance Verification
"He was scheduled" is not "He attended." Without verified attendance, the agency can't prove the officer was actually present.
How BrassOps Solves It
Scores = verified attendance
Every score logged is proof the officer was there, fired, and was evaluated by an instructor. No gap between scheduled and attended.
Failure
5. No Performance Metrics
Pass/fail with no scoring data is a weak defense. Courts want measurable competency — not a checkbox that says "completed."
How BrassOps Solves It
Granular data + AI diagnostics
Real scores, behavioral analytics, and AI shot analysis. Every session has measurable data that demonstrates genuine competency evaluation.

The Legal Standard
What Courts Are Really Evaluating
To win a negligent training case, agencies must prove all four of these. Documentation is the only way. BrassOps handles every one.
1
Training Existed
Every qual session, dry-fire practice, and training event logged with time-stamped records proving the program was active.
2
Training Was Appropriate
Courses of fire, lesson plans, and coaching notes document what was taught and why — tied to duties and deficiencies.
3
Officer Received It
Score-based attendance verification proves the officer was physically present and participated. No ambiguity.
4
Officer Showed Competency
Granular scores, pass/fail thresholds, behavioral analytics, and AI diagnostics prove measurable competency.

Documentation is the only way to prove all four. Paper records fail under scrutiny. Spreadsheets get lost. Institutional memory retires. BrassOps creates an automatic, continuous, defensible record that protects your agency — before you ever need it.
