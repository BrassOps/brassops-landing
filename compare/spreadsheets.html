What Changes When You Switch

Qualification Tracking

With Spreadsheets

Enter scores manually. Calculate pass/fail yourself. Track expirations by memory or calendar reminders.

With BrassOps

Log from your phone on the firing line. Pass/fail auto-calculated against your thresholds. Expirations tracked with automated alerts.

Remedial Training

With Spreadsheets

No automated process. You have to remember who failed, create a plan manually, and follow up yourself. If you forget, nobody catches it.

With BrassOps

Auto remedial enrollment the moment a score fails. Structured workflow with coaching plan, milestones, and escalation. Nothing missed.

Officer Readiness

With Spreadsheets

Open the spreadsheet. Find the right tab. Cross-reference dates. Do the math. Hope the data is current.

With BrassOps

Open the dashboard. Green, yellow, red for every officer. Filter by unit, shift, or rank. Always current.

Weapon Tracking

With Spreadsheets

Separate spreadsheet for weapons. Manually cross-reference which officer has which serial. No link between weapon and qualification data.

With BrassOps

Every weapon tracked by serial. Linked to officer assignments. Qualification data tied to specific weapons. Accuracy trends per firearm.

Audit Response

With Spreadsheets

Scramble to compile records from multiple tabs, files, and folders. Hope nothing is missing. Takes days.

With BrassOps

Generate a complete training history for any officer in seconds. Every record time-stamped and searchable.

Command Staff Visibility

With Spreadsheets

Command staff has zero visibility unless the range master manually compiles a report. They only see what you show them, when you show them.

With BrassOps

Real-time dashboards accessible to command staff at any time. No reports to request. No waiting.
