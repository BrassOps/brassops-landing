Your New System

What Changes for Range Masters

Every feature built to give you back your time and protect your program.

Qualification Tracking on Autopilot

Log sessions from your phone. Pass/fail auto-calculated. Expirations tracked. Alerts fire at 90, 60, and 30 days. You never manually check a date again.

Auto Remedial Enrollment

Officer fails? Remedial case creates itself instantly. Structured workflow with coaching plan, milestones, and escalation. Zero manual intervention. Zero missed cases.

Officer Readiness Scoring

Every officer carries a readiness score and a grade from Elite Ready down to Critical. One engine watches practice accuracy, a second watches qualification performance, and between them they flag score drops, repeat failures, and safety concerns before you would have spotted them. No report to compile, the dashboard is the report.

Behavioral Scoring

Nine range behaviors rated every session, from muzzle discipline to reload habits to response to coaching. Individually it tells you who needs work. Rolled up, it tells you whether twelve officers failing the same drill is twelve personnel problems or one curriculum problem.

Armory & Weapon Management

Every firearm tracked by serial. Officer assignments. Inspection scheduling with due-date alerts. Full repair history. Weapon performance monitoring links accuracy to specific firearms.

Automated Alerts

Expiration warnings, remedial milestones, officer flagging, escalation notifications, all sent to the right people automatically. The system watches so you don't have to.

Audit-Ready Records

Every qualification, every remedial case, every coaching note, time-stamped and searchable. Complete training history for any officer generated in seconds. Not days. Seconds.
