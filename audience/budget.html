What BrassOps Does, In Plain Terms

No jargon. Here's what the software does and why it matters to your municipality.

Automatic Record-Keeping

Every time an officer qualifies with their firearm, the score, date, weapon, and instructor are recorded digitally, automatically. No paper forms. No manual data entry. No records that can be lost or destroyed.

Automatic Compliance Monitoring

The system continuously tracks which officers are current on qualifications and which are approaching expiration. Alerts sent automatically. No officer can quietly fall out of compliance.

Automatic Lawsuit Protection

If your department is sued over a training issue, BrassOps produces a complete, time-stamped training record for any officer in minutes. This is the documentation that wins cases, or prevents them from being filed.

Automatic Accountability

Department leadership sees real-time training status for every officer without requesting reports. Full transparency into the program, accessible at any time from any device.
