Features & Automations Reference
A quick-reference guide for sales conversations. Each section explains what the feature does, what it automates, and why it matters to the agency.
Qualification Tracking
The backbone of BrassOps. Every firearms qualification session is logged with scores, dates, weapon used, course of fire, and instructor. The system automatically calculates pass/fail against the agency's configured thresholds and maintains a time-stamped record for every officer.
  • Scores logged per officer with full session metadata (date, weapon, course, instructor)
  • Pass/fail auto-calculated against agency-defined qualification thresholds
  • Qualification expiration dates tracked automatically — no manual date math
  • Complete historical record for every officer, searchable and exportable
Automation
When a score is logged, the system instantly determines pass/fail status, updates the officer's qualification expiration date, and recalculates their readiness status on the department dashboard — all without any manual steps.
Auto Remedial Enrollment
When an officer fails a qualification, BrassOps automatically creates a remedial training case and enrolls the officer — the moment the score is logged. No range master action required. The remedial workflow includes issue tracking, coaching notes, progress milestones, and escalation paths.
  • Triggered immediately on qualification failure — zero delay, zero manual steps
  • Remedial case created with structured workflow: issue identification, coaching plan, re-qualification
  • Instructors assigned automatically or manually to remedial cases
  • Escalation paths for officers who don't meet remedial milestones within set timeframes
  • Every step time-stamped and documented for legal defensibility
Automation
The entire pipeline is hands-free. Score logged → fail detected → remedial case created → officer notified → instructor assigned → training tracked until re-qualification. If escalation thresholds are breached, command staff is alerted automatically.
Automated Alerts & Notifications
BrassOps continuously monitors qualification dates, remedial progress, and officer status across the entire department. When something needs attention, the right people are notified automatically — before it becomes a problem.
  • Qualification expiration warnings sent at configurable intervals (e.g. 90, 60, 30 days out)
  • Remedial case status alerts when milestones are due or overdue
  • Officer flagging notifications sent to range masters and command staff
  • Configurable notification channels and recipients per alert type
Automation
No one has to remember to check spreadsheets. The system monitors every officer's status continuously and pushes alerts to the right people at the right time. Qualifications can't silently lapse.

Officer Readiness Monitoring
A real-time, color-coded dashboard showing the qualification and training status of every officer in the department. Green (qualified), yellow (expiring soon), red (non-compliant or in remedial). Command staff and range masters see the same live picture without generating reports.
  • Department-wide readiness view updated in real time as scores and dates change
  • Filter by unit, shift, rank, or status to drill into specific groups
  • Individual officer detail pages with full qualification history and trend data
Automation
Readiness status recalculates automatically whenever a qualification is logged, an expiration date passes, or a remedial case changes state. The dashboard is always current — no one has to update it.
AI-Powered Shot Analysis
Officers or instructors upload a photo of a target, and BrassOps AI analyzes the shot grouping to provide an instant diagnostic — including probable cause of dispersion and recommended corrective drills. It's like having an expert coach evaluate every target.
  • Upload a target photo from any device (phone camera on the firing line works great)
  • AI identifies shot pattern, grouping tightness, and point-of-impact shift
  • Returns probable cause (trigger control, flinch, grip, sight alignment, etc.)
  • Suggests specific corrective drills tailored to the identified issue
  • Analysis saved to the officer's profile for tracking improvement over time
Performance Analytics & Behavioral Scoring
Deep analytics that go beyond basic pass/fail. BrassOps tracks performance trends over time at both the individual officer and department-wide level. Behavioral scoring identifies specific strengths and weaknesses — giving leadership the data to design targeted training rather than one-size-fits-all programs.
  • Per-officer score trends, session history, and improvement tracking
  • Department-wide statistics: average scores, qualification rates, common deficiencies
  • Behavioral scoring surfaces patterns (e.g. consistent trigger issues across multiple officers)
  • Drill leaderboards and achievement badges to drive engagement and healthy competition
  • Data exportable for reports to command staff, oversight, or budget justifications
Automation
Analytics update in real time as new data flows in. Behavioral scores recalculate after every session. Leaderboards and badges are awarded automatically when officers hit milestones — no admin configuration needed.

Armory — Weapon Inventory Management
A complete weapon fleet management system. Every department-issued firearm is tracked from acquisition to retirement — including which officer it's assigned to, when it was last inspected, and its full repair history.
  • Full inventory with serial numbers, make/model, and acquisition date
  • Officer assignment tracking — know who has what at all times
  • Inspection scheduling with due-date tracking and completion logging
  • Repair history log tied to each weapon for lifecycle management
Automation
Inspection due dates are tracked automatically and surface alerts when weapons are due. Assignment changes are logged with timestamps for full chain-of-custody documentation.
Weapon-Specific Performance Monitoring
BrassOps links qualification and training data directly to the specific weapon used during each session. This creates a per-weapon performance profile that tracks accuracy trends, round counts, and reliability over time — giving armory staff and leadership hard data on how individual firearms are performing in the field.
  • Every qualification session records which specific weapon (by serial number) was used
  • Per-weapon accuracy trends — identify firearms that may be affecting officer performance
  • Round count tracking tied to each weapon for maintenance scheduling and lifespan planning
  • Reliability flagging — surface weapons with recurring issues or declining performance patterns
  • Cross-reference weapon data with officer data to separate shooter issues from equipment issues
Automation
Weapon performance profiles build automatically as sessions are logged. If a specific firearm shows declining accuracy across multiple officers, or if round count thresholds are reached, the system flags it for review — no manual analysis required.
Officer / Instructor Pairing System
BrassOps pairs officers with specific instructors based on training needs, skill gaps, and instructor specialties. This creates a structured coaching relationship — not just random assignment — so remedial training and ongoing development are led by the right person for each officer's situation.
  • Officers paired with instructors based on identified deficiencies and instructor expertise
  • Pairing history maintained — full record of who trained whom and when
  • Instructors see their paired officers' complete profiles, scores, and behavioral data
  • Continuity of coaching: the same instructor follows an officer through remedial to re-qualification
Automation
When an officer enters remedial, the system can auto-assign an instructor based on availability and specialization. Pairing history is logged automatically, and instructors are notified of new assignments and status changes for their paired officers.

Instructor Tools
Assign instructors to officers, sessions, or remedial cases. Instructors can log coaching notes, track officer progress over time, and see their full assigned roster with status indicators.
  • Instructor assignment to sessions and remedial cases
  • Coaching notes tied to officers with full history
  • Instructor workload visibility for training coordinators
Dry-Fire Training
Officers log dry-fire practice sessions between range days. Sessions are tracked with duration, drill type, and notes. Achievement badges unlock at milestones to keep officers engaged and building consistent habits.
  • Structured session logging with drill type and duration
  • Progress tracking visible to officers and instructors
  • Achievement badges awarded automatically at milestones
Range Ops
Operational management for the range itself — scheduling, automations, inventory, and day-of logistics. Designed to streamline the admin side so the team can focus on running training.
  • Range day scheduling and calendar management
  • Ammo and supply inventory tracking
  • Configurable automations for recurring range workflows
BrassLink Community
A built-in community platform connecting firearms instructors across agencies nationwide. Every BrassOps user gets access — it's a retention driver and a genuine value-add.
  • N.A.R.M.S. — Anonymous cross-agency benchmarking
  • MythBusters — Data-driven debunking of training myths
  • Training Resources — Shared drills, studies, and best practices
  • Instructor Assessment — Peer-informed evaluation framework
  • Equipment Reviews — Honest, unsponsored gear reviews
Security & Access
Law enforcement-grade security designed for sensitive training data. No special hardware or installs — web-based, works on any device.
  • AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS in transit
  • Role-based access for officers, instructors, range masters, command staff
  • Full audit log of every action for legal defensibility
  • U.S.-based cloud hosting with redundancy
  • Agency data fully isolated — no cross-agency visibility
